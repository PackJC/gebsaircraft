[h1]Mod Information[/h1]
Geb's Aircraft adds flyable planes to DayZ, in multiplayer. Each one is simulated as a real aircraft: lift and drag on every wing panel and tail surface, propeller thrust, torque and slipstream, sprung wheels, an engine you start and fuel you refill. Take off from a field in a Super Cub, fly a Bf 109 fighter with its gear, flaps and working cockpit, or put either one on floats and fly from the sea.

It adds [b]4 aircraft[/b], each in [b]3 paint schemes[/b] (standard, winter and desert), and their spare main wheels.

[b]Website (how to fly each plane, server setup and every classname):[/b] [url=https://packjc.github.io/gebsaircraft/]packjc.github.io/gebsaircraft[/url]

[h1]The Aircraft[/h1]
[list]
[*] [b]Piper PA-18 Super Cub:[/b] a two-seat tandem bush plane on fat tyres. It takes off from a field, cruises at 160-180 km/h and lands slow. It flies like a real one: it stalls, it can spin, it swings on the takeoff run, and braked too hard it noses over. The door opens, the passenger rides behind you.
[*] [b]Messerschmitt Bf 109 E-3:[/b] the single-seat fighter of 1940. A DB 601 on a constant-speed propeller, retractable gear on G, flaps on a handwheel, slats that slide out by themselves near the stall, an inertia starter you hear wind up, and a working cockpit with fifteen German instruments. About 480 km/h flat out and 16 m/s climb. No guns yet.
[*] [b]Bf 109 W on floats:[/b] the same fighter on twin floats, flown from the sea and the lakes. Its gear is folded away for good. Plane it over the hump and off the water at about 170 km/h.
[*] [b]Super Cub on floats, the easy one:[/b] flown arcade. The keys turn it at a steady rate and the plane flies itself: let go and it levels its wings and holds its height, hold A or D and it banks into a turn without rudder, hold S and it climbs. It can't stall or spin, lifts itself off the water at full throttle and lands itself with the throttle closed.
[/list]

[h1]Features[/h1]
[list]
[*] [b]A real flight model:[/b] every wing panel and tail surface gets its own airflow, so rolling, yawing and the stall come out of the air itself. A wing that stalls first drops. Ground effect near the ground and the water.
[*] [b]Propellers with character:[/b] thrust that falls off with speed, torque that rolls the plane, a corkscrew slipstream on the fin, and on the 109 the propeller's gyroscopic kick.
[*] [b]Real wheels and floats:[/b] each wheel rides a spring and grips up to its load; the tailwheel steers on the pedals and toe brakes help you turn. Each float is simulated in slices: buoyancy, planing on the step, the hump of the takeoff run, water rudders on the pedals, spray and the sound of the water.
[*] [b]Engines you look after:[/b] start them with the start action, they run on their own fuel, refill them from a jerrycan, and a crash damages the engine or the airframe where it hits. A propeller in the water drowns the engine.
[*] [b]An instrument panel on the HUD:[/b] airspeed, height, throttle, rpm, fuel, flaps, trim and the gear.
[*] [b]Smooth in multiplayer:[/b] the plane is simulated the same on the server and the pilot's machine, from the pilot's controls, through DayZ's own vehicle physics.
[*] [b]Swim up and climb in:[/b] the floatplanes can be boarded from the water.
[*] [b]Winter and desert camouflage:[/b] every plane in three schemes. The 109s are whitewashed for the winter or painted in tropical sand and olive with a white fuselage band; the Cubs come in white and grey, or sand and brown. The floats are painted to match.
[/list]

[h1]Controls[/h1]
All can be rebound under Settings > Controls > Geb's Aircraft.
[list]
[*] [b]W / S:[/b] stick forward / back
[*] [b]A / D:[/b] roll left / right
[*] [b]Z / C:[/b] rudder (and the tailwheel and water rudders)
[*] [b]Shift / Ctrl:[/b] throttle up / down
[*] [b]X / R:[/b] flaps down / up
[*] [b]Page Up / Page Down:[/b] trim
[*] [b]Space:[/b] wheel brakes
[*] [b]G:[/b] landing gear (Bf 109 E-3)
[*] Start the engine by holding the start action; hold F near the cockpit to get in.
[/list]
